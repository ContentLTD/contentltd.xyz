The Brief

A fandom with an identity of its own

Fan communities need visibility and an identity. For Hamilton Enjoyers, the goal was to build a distinct brand while showcasing what the Discord server had to offer.

The community needed something beyond the usual server listing: a website that would help it stand out and give new members a feel for the community before they joined.

The Struggle

From Discord to a home on the web

Within a week of joining the Hamilton Enjoyers staff team, I built the community a website. The challenge was to create a unique static site that gave the server a real online presence beyond Discord.

I built on what I learned from making ACRP’s site, bringing those skills into a new design with thoughtful UI and UX. The aim was to make the community’s personality come through while keeping the experience clear and easy to navigate.

The Stack

HTML. CSS. Vanilla JS, baby.

Just like last time, I kept it simple: HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. A familiar stack, with room to build something that felt like Hamilton Enjoyers.

HTML5 CSS3 Vanilla JS

The Outcome

A website the community could be proud of

The result is a website that feels great to use, is easy to understand, and gives Hamilton Enjoyers a distinct presence beyond Discord. It’s a home on the web that the community is proud to be associated with.
